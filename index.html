LedgerMate — Financial Management Platform

Bookkeeping & Automated M-Pesa Reconciliation for Kenyan Businesses

Informal traders and SMEs in Kenya lose hours manually reconciling mobile-money payments with sales records. LedgerMate automates transaction capture, inventory costing, and financial reporting in one lightweight tool.
